Solution:
    Since S1 has three terms, we we have 3 succ terms, 3 pred terms, 3 iszero terms, and 27 if then else terms in S2 
    in addition to everything in S1. This creates a total of 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 27 = 39 terms.

    For S3, 39 succ terms, 39 pred terms, 39 is zero terms and 39^3 if then else terms, this in addition to everything in S2.
    So we have
    |S3| =  39 + 39 + 39 + 39 + 39^3  = 59475
